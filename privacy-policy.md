# Privacy Policy — Common Ground Free Store Mobile App

**Effective date:** July 23, 2026
**Last updated:** July 23, 2026

Common Ground Free Store ("we," "us") operates the Common Ground Free Store mobile app (the
"App"). This policy explains what the App collects, why, and who it is shared with.

We built the App to do one job: let a family register a device once, check in when they arrive at
the store, and see their remaining monthly allocations. We collect only what is needed for that.

## Information we collect

When you register a device, the App asks for:

- **Last name**
- **Date of birth**
- **Mobile phone number**

These three items are sent to our Salesforce system solely to **match you to an existing record**
that store staff created when you first enrolled at the store. The App does not create new
accounts and does not enroll new families — if there is no matching record, registration simply
fails.

### Phone verification

Your mobile phone number is also sent to **Google Firebase Authentication**, which sends you a
one-time SMS verification code to confirm the number belongs to you. We do this so that someone
who happens to know your name and birth date cannot register a device in your name.

### Device identifier

After successful registration, our system issues a random **device identifier (GUID)** that is
stored securely on your device (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) and sent with later requests to
identify your device. It is not your phone number and cannot be used to identify you outside our
system.

## What we do NOT collect

The App does not collect, use, or transmit:

- Location data
- Contacts, photos, camera, microphone, or files
- Advertising identifiers
- Analytics, usage tracking, or behavioral profiling

The App contains **no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking software**.

## How we use your information

Only to:

1. Match your device to your existing record at the store
2. Verify your phone number by SMS
3. Generate the check-in code you present at the store
4. Show your household's remaining monthly allocations

We do **not** sell, rent, or share your information for advertising or marketing.

## Who we share it with

| Service | Purpose | Data involved |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google Firebase Authentication** | Sends the SMS verification code | Mobile phone number |
| **Salesforce** | Our system of record for enrolled families | Last name, date of birth, phone number, device identifier |

Both are service providers acting on our behalf. Their handling of data is governed by their own
privacy policies:

- Google/Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Salesforce: https://www.salesforce.com/company/privacy/

We do not share your information with anyone else except where required by law.

## Data retention

Your record is retained in our Salesforce system for as long as you are an active participant at
the store, in line with our records-retention practices as a charitable organization. The device
identifier stored on your phone is removed when you uninstall the App, and can be invalidated by
store staff at any time (for example, if your phone is lost or replaced).

## Security

Information is transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. The device identifier is held in the
operating system's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). The App does not store your
name, date of birth, or phone number on the device after registration completes.

## Children's privacy

The App is intended to be used by **adult heads of household**. It is not directed at children,
and we do not knowingly collect information directly from children through the App. Household
members, including children, may be recorded in our store records by staff during in-person
enrollment — that information is not collected by the App.

## Your choices

You may:

- **Stop using the App** and uninstall it at any time, which removes the device identifier from
  your phone.
- **Ask us what we hold about you**, ask for corrections, or ask us to delete your record, by
  contacting us below. Because our records are maintained by store staff, these requests are
  handled in person or by contacting the store directly.
